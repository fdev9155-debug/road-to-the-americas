A game controller also works: left stick steers, right trigger is gas, left trigger is brake.

Touch screen

Slide your left thumb along the strip to steer. Right thumb handles gas and brake, and the + and − buttons shift gears. The controls appear the first time you touch the screen.

Updates

What changed, newest first.

  1. Nov 19, 2026

    Play in your browser

    • The game now runs on this site on phones, tablets and computers.
    • Touch controls: steering strip, gas, brake, shifting, pause, camera, pit and data buttons.
    • On the web the game adjusts its 3D resolution during a race to hold a smooth frame rate.
    • Smaller first download, and the game is saved on your device after the first play.
  2. Nov 17, 2026

    Weather, damage and a new look

    • Weather: clear, overcast, rain and thunderstorm with lightning, plus a day and night cycle.
    • Quick Race setup: laps, AI difficulty, field size, weather, time of day and damage level.
    • Crash damage: bumpers dent, hang and fall off, wheels can break away, and losing a wheel retires you from the race.
    • Faster, more competitive AI with four skill levels.
    • New main menu, pause menu, launch intro and broadcast-style race HUD.
    • Spotter calls for the green flag, traffic, rain and wheel loss.
